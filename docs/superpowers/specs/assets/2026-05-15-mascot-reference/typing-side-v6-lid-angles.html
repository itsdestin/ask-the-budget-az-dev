Sleek aluminum — six lid angles

Same mascot, hand, and laptop body from v4d. Six different lid open angles, from "just cracked" to "way reclined." Pick the angle that feels right for the typing state.

A — Just cracked (~45°)

Lid hard-leaned forward, almost reading-it-from-above. Reads "deeply focused" or "just opening it."

B — Cozy forward (~75°)

Slight forward lean, like leaning over to read. Comfortable but engaged.

C — Mostly upright (~95°)

Standard typing angle, lid nearly vertical. Most "office laptop" of the bunch.

D — Comfortable (~110°)

Slight back lean — classic typing-while-relaxed angle. v4 was here.

E — Reclined (~125°)

Casual back-lean. Reads "thinking, not actively typing right now."

F — Way back (~140°)

Almost cinema mode. Lid laid back, less screen face visible — most silver. Reads "letting it work, watching from afar."

D was the v4 angle — comfortable for most users. C reads as "office serious." A and B are the most stylistically distinct (forward-lean is unusual). E and F suggest a more relaxed state. Pick one — or pair two angles for an animation (e.g. "lid opens from B → settles at D").
