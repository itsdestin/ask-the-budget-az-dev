Idle "moments" — pick the ones that feel right

Short pixel-art beats that fire at random intervals (every 8–15s) while the mascot is idle. Each card auto-replays its moment every ~4s so you can see what it does. Click multiple — multi-select. The base idle (subtle bob + occasional blink) is always on; these are the flavor on top.

Blink

Eyes briefly close. Baseline; should fire every ~3-6s regardless.

Look sideways

Eyes shift right and back. Curious / checking something.

Push up glasses

Right hand briefly raises to nose, glasses shift up a notch. Very analyst.

Sip coffee

Mug appears at waist, raises to mouth, gone. Subtle, recurring.

Stretch arms

Both arms raise overhead briefly. Rare — like, once every 30s.

Quick smile

Mouth briefly widens. Subtle warmth, no obvious trigger.

Lightbulb moment

Bulb + sparkles appear above head briefly. Most "narrative" of the bunch.

Tip cap

Cap briefly lowers / shifts forward. Like adjusting the hat.

Multi-select — click as many as you want. Recommended subset: Blink + Look sideways + Push up glasses as a calm baseline; add Sip coffee or Lightbulb if you want more narrative flavor. All are pure pixel-art frame swaps, no smooth tweening.
