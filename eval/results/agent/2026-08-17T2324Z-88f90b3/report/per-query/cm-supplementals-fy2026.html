The JLBC Baseline had earlier projected a smaller GF shortfall of **$64,528,400** plus **$340,175,800** in expenditure authority . **What drove it:** The enacted FY 2026 budget assumed enrollment would grow by ~15,000 members. Instead, enrollment *declined* by 107,000 in the first six months, projected to end at 1.68 million (164,000 below June 2025). That caseload drop generated ~(104,672,500) in GF savings . But those savings were overwhelmed by capitation rate increases: AHCCCS implemented a 5.5% average increase on October 1, 2025 (vs. the 3.0% budgeted assumption), then announced a retroactive increase pushing the combined adjustment to an estimated 14.4%. That generated **$157,908,300** in GF costs above the enacted budget . AHCCCS cited three factors: sicker members remaining enrolled as healthier ones left, significant increases in Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) service utilization, and general medical inflation . ### 2.
