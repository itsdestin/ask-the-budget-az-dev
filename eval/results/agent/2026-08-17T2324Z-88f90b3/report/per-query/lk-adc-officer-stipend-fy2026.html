In FY 2027, the one-time stipend was removed (the $17,075,300 was backed out of ADC) and replaced with an **ongoing 4% salary increase** of $20,267,100 — converting the one-time bonus into permanent pay .

All set. The per-officer amount wasn't a flat dollar figure — it was simply **4% of each individual officer's annual salary**, spread evenly through every pay period. So an officer earning, say, $55,000/year would have seen about $2,200 added over the course of FY 2026 (~$85 per biweekly check). The total appropriation ($21.4M across ADC, the private-prison per-diem line, and DJC) was the aggregate needed to cover that 4% across the entire correctional-officer workforce.
